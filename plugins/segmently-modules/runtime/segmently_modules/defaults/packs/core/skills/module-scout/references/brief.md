# The brief (step 6)

Shown in the conversation, short — it feeds a discussion, not a plan:

- per module: what it owns (a line or two); the relevant child; the constraints that bear on the
  topic, quoted short with their doc;
- the auto-pulled modules, listed under the module that pulled them with the edge type, so the
  user sees why they came;
- missing docs and stubs (with the successor read);
- **Suggested next reads:** role docs, `complements` targets and reverse-edge modules worth
  loading, children not loaded — and `module-discover` once the task is confirmed,
  `module-research` for a question that needs the code.
